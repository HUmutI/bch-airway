The trained weights distributed in the releases of this repository
(`airmorph_seg.tar.gz` and `medpseg_airway.tar.gz`: the five AirMorph-R
networks and MEDPSeg-R) were trained on the ATM'22/ATM'26 challenge training
data, which are provided under CC BY-NC terms and a data usage agreement that
limits use to academic, non-commercial research.

- Research and other non-commercial use, modification and redistribution are

- Commercial use is not permitted.
- Any publication, preprint, presentation or derivative work that uses these
  weights must cite the ATM challenge paper:
  Zhang, M., et al. Multi-site, multi-domain airway tree modeling.
  Medical Image Analysis, 90:102957, 2023. doi:10.1016/j.media.2023.102957.
  Once the official ATM'26 challenge paper is available, cite it as well.

The ATM data themselves are not redistributed here; they are available from
https://atm26.grand-challenge.org/ under the challenge's own agreement.
