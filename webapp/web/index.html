Upload a chest CT

One NIfTI volume (.nii or .nii.gz) in Hounsfield units. The pipeline segments the airway with three models, fuses them, and names every branch by lobe and segment. A typical scan takes about ten minutes; a GPU is started for it when you upload.

Drop a file here, or click to choose

What you get back

The fused airway mask, the lobar and segmental label maps, the branch instances and the centreline, all as NIfTI in the orientation of the scan you uploaded, plus an in-browser 3D view, per-branch morphometry with percentiles for age, sanity checks on the result, and a bundle that opens in ITK-SNAP for correction. Files are kept on the server only as long as needed to serve them.

Method: union of AirMorph, MEDPSeg and TfeNet with seeded component selection, then a graph transformer for branch naming. Research prototype.
